# Demoblaze — purchase flow (example requirements)

> Example project for the archetype. Removed by `remove-example.bat`.

Source: E2E automation exercise on https://www.demoblaze.com/ — "functional automated test (E2E) of a purchase flow".

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| PUR-01 | A customer can add two products to the cart from their product pages. Each addition is acknowledged ("Product added"). |
| PUR-02 | The cart lists every added product with its title and price, and the total equals the sum of the prices. |
| PUR-03 | The customer completes the purchase form (Name, Country, City, Credit card, Month, Year). Name and Credit card are mandatory: "Please fill out Name and Creditcard." |
| PUR-04 | Purchasing shows "Thank you for your purchase!" with the order Id, the Amount (= cart total, USD), the Card Number, the Name and the Date (today). |
